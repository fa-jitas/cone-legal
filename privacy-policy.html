Privacy Policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

1.Information we collect

Account information. When you create a Cone account, we collect your email address and password. Your password is handled entirely by our authentication provider (Supabase) and is never visible to us in plain text.

Pet profile. Your pet's name, species, gender, breed, birth date, weight, and an optional photo, if you choose to add one.

Receipts you submit. When you log a price — the clinic name, procedure, amount paid, your pet's species, the date, and an optional photo of the receipt.

Location. With your permission, your device's location, used only to show distance to nearby vets and sort results by how close they are. You can decline this and still use Cone — you just won't get distance-based sorting.

Camera and photo library. Used only when you actively choose to take or select a photo for your pet's profile or a receipt. We never access these without your action.

2.How we use it

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To show your pet's profile back to you.
  • To show nearby vets, sorted by distance when location is available.
  • To display crowdsourced pricing so other pet owners can see what procedures typically cost at a given clinic.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for advertising.

3.What's public vs. private

Cone's core idea is price transparency, so some information is intentionally visible to other users:

  • Public: the clinic name, procedure, price, and pet species from a receipt you submit. This is what lets other pet owners compare prices.
  • Private, visible only to you: your pet's profile and photo, your email address, and any photo you attach to a receipt. Other users never see your receipt photo or your pet's photo.

4.Third-party services

Cone is built on Supabase, which provides our account system, database, and photo storage. Supabase processes data on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes. We don't use any advertising or analytics networks.

5.Retention & deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account, right in the app, at any time:

Profile → Settings → Account → Delete Account. This immediately and permanently removes your pet profile, your receipts, and your account itself. This cannot be undone.

6.Children's privacy

Cone is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

7.Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

8.Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at cone.vetfinder@gmail.com.
