Our mission

Vet costs are one of the biggest unknowns in owning a pet — the same procedure can cost wildly different amounts from one clinic to the next, and there's no real way to find out beforehand. Cone exists to close that gap with real numbers from real pet owners, not estimates.

We're starting in the Bay Area, built around one simple idea: the more people who share what they actually paid, the easier it gets for the next pet owner to budget for their visit, choose a clinic with confidence, and never be caught off guard at checkout.
